# Sprint 2 – Development: Collecting and Analysing the Data

*Moreux Camp Map – NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026*

**Goal:** Gather the layers for Moreux, understand what each one shows and decide where to put the camp.

## What we did

- Collected the backdrops from Mars Trek: CTX images (optical), THEMIS day and night infrared (thermal) and the MOLA–HRSC elevation model (topographic).
- Added the water layers: glacier-like forms, supraglacial and proglacial valleys, gullies, hydrous minerals and aqueous minerals, plus the dune field and NASA's exploration zone.
- Wrote a description of every layer: what it does, what it shows and how it helps the crew.
- Tested our camp idea (the red dot) against slope, night temperature, distance to the dunes and distance to the ice.

## Problems

Some layers had no records in our area (recurring slope lineae and sulfates). The night thermal image did not line up with the other layers, and the red marker in our screenshots changed the elevation values under it.

## Solutions

We kept only layers with data in Moreux, aligned the night image using the map labels and removed the marker area from the elevation data.

## Result

Base camp at 41.2° N, 44.4° E: flat (0.4°), firm ground that stays warm at night, 7.8 km from the dunes and 22.5 km by route from the nearest glacier.
