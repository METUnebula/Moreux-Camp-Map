# Sprint 4 – Final: Testing, Publishing and the Demo

*Moreux Camp Map – NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026*

**Goal:** Check everything, put the project online and prepare all the submission materials.

## What we did

- Checked our layer document against the data and corrected the mistakes we found.
- Published the map on Netlify and uploaded the code to GitHub.
- Wrote the English project page for the Space Apps submission form.
- Prepared a 7-slide presentation, a 30-second demo video for NASA and a 1-minute video for our local coordinator.
- Wrote the Interplanetary Survival Guide: hazards, base camp, a pre-Marswalk checklist, route rules, emergency procedures and water from ice.

## Problems

At first the Netlify site was visible only to our team, and one video file was too large to open. Because our team members are under 18, we could not use our own voices or faces in the videos.

## Solutions

We made the site public, made a smaller video file and used on-screen English captions instead of narration.

## Result

The map is live at https://stupendous-pithivier-f91637.netlify.app and the project is ready to submit before the deadline (15 November 2026, 23:59).
