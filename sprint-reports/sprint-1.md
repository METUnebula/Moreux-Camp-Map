# Sprint 1 – Getting Started: Research and Choosing the Site

*Moreux Camp Map – NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026*

**Goal:** Understand the challenge, set a scientific goal and choose where on Mars to work.

## What we did

- Registered for Space Apps, read the challenge and opened all the NASA resources it lists: Mars Trek, USGS, the Curiosity route, Worldview and the Perseverance raw images.
- Divided the work between our six team members.
- Set our scientific goal: finding water sources using optical, topographic and thermal data.
- Compared Gale crater (Mount Sharp) with Deuteronilus Mensae. Gale is well studied but has little ice, so we started with Deuteronilus Mensae and looked for the SWIM ice maps.
- Searched for regions that have water evidence and all three data types, then moved to Protonilus Mensae and finally to Moreux crater.

## Problems

The SWIM maps were not available as a layer in Mars Trek, and our first region did not have every layer we needed.

## Result

We chose Moreux crater in Protonilus Mensae, which has glaciers, gullies, valleys and water-related minerals inside one crater.
